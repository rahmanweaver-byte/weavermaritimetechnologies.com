We connect transitioning Marines, family members, and veterans to educational resources, employment opportunities, and services that support their career and life goals.

It is free, there is nothing to enroll in, and it does not expire. A representative in your region connects you to the people who can actually help — employers, county veterans offices, schools, housing, VA. Whether you EAS next spring or got out in 2009, pick whichever of us covers where you are and reach out.

Veterans Resources
Nationwide & statewide
Marine For Life Network (Official)
The national program page. Marines, veterans, and family members can request a referral to their regional representative using the form at the bottom of that page. It routes to whoever covers where you are landing.
Marine For Life Network on LinkedIn
The program's own page, plus the groups behind it — one national group, four regional ones, and several built around particular industries. Southern California and San Diego both sit in the West region. Worth joining before you need it: the point of a network is that it already exists when the question comes up.
DD-214 — Request Your Separation Document
The document every other door on this page asks for. Free from the National Archives through their eVetRecs tool — you'll verify your identity with ID.me first. Ask for the Member-4 copy: it shows character of service, which is the version VA and employers actually want. Never pay a company for this. The Archives provides it free, and if you're filing for VA benefits, VA pulls your DD-214 for you.
milConnect — Recently Separated
If you left service in the last few years, your records may still sit with your branch rather than the National Archives, and milConnect is the faster route. It's also where a Post-9/11 GI Bill transfer to a spouse or child is requested — and that has to be approved while you are still serving, so handle it before your CAC goes away.
Staying In — Active Duty to the Marine Corps Reserve
If you do nothing at your EAS you land in the Individual Ready Reserve by default — non-drilling, no pay, no points, still recallable until your eight-year obligation is up. The drilling reserve, the SMCR, is something you have to go and ask for. Prior Service Recruiting handles affiliation: mcrcpsr@marines.usmc.mil. Ask about the Direct Affiliation Program, which moves you straight from active duty into a reserve billet, and about affiliation bonuses for short MOSs. Start this before you separate — re-affiliating after a break is a longer road than never leaving.
What Drilling Actually Looks Like
One weekend a month and an annual training period of two weeks to a month, at a reserve center near where you live — units sit in 48 states, DC, and Puerto Rico. What it buys you beyond the paycheck: retirement points that keep counting toward a 20-year letter, access to TRICARE Reserve Select at a fraction of civilian premiums, and MGIB-SR eligibility. Ask your career planner for the Reserve Opportunities and Obligations Brief while you still have a chain of command to ask.
VA — Benefits, Healthcare & Claims
Start here for healthcare enrollment, disability claims, education benefits, and home loans. Set up a VA.gov account with Login.gov or ID.me and you can file claims, track status, and pull your own records in one place.
DoW SkillBridge — Transition Internships
Train with a civilian employer during your last 180 days on active duty while still drawing full pay, BAH, and TRICARE. Commander approval is required and every branch now caps the length by rank, so open the conversation 9 to 12 months out. Drilling reservists generally don't qualify unless on 180+ day Title 10 orders.
VR&E — Veteran Readiness & Employment
Chapter 31. Tuition, training, and job placement for veterans with a service-connected disability that gets in the way of working. Broader than the GI Bill — it can fund retraining, equipment, or self-employment — and it doesn't spend your GI Bill months.
CalVet — California Veteran Benefits
California benefits stacked on top of federal ones: property tax exemptions for disabled veterans, college fee waivers for dependents, veterans homes, and CalVet home and farm loans. California residency required.
CalVet College Fee Waiver — Veterans' Kids
Tuition-free at any California Community College, CSU or UC — it waives systemwide tuition and fees, not books or housing. Plan A has no income limit: the parent is rated 100% or died of a service-connected cause, and that happened before the child turned 21; the child must be over 14 and under 27 and can't also use VA Chapter 35. Plan B takes any rating from 0% to 100% with no age limit, but the student's own income plus parental support must stay under $22,941 for 2026–27. Apply free at your county veterans service office.
San Diego County
County Office of Military & Veterans Affairs
Accredited county representatives who prepare and file your VA claims for free, follow them to a decision, and build appeals. Walk-in centers around the county. San Diego has the second largest veteran population in California — use them instead of paying a claims agent.
Workshops for Warriors — Job Training
A nonprofit school at 2970 Main Street in San Diego running accelerated 16-week CNC machining and welding programs. Graduates earn credentials from the American Welding Society, NIMS, Mastercam, and SolidWorks. Open to veterans, transitioning service members, and wounded warriors; VA education benefits accepted. (619) 550-1620.
Riverside County
Riverside County Veterans' Services
Established in 1930 and serving roughly 150,000 county veterans. Free benefits counseling, claims preparation, and appeals from accredited county representatives, plus referrals for medical care, education, and employment. Main office at 4360 Orange Street in Riverside, with satellite offices countywide.
Los Angeles County
LA County Military & Veterans Affairs
County advocates for veterans, dependents, and survivors on benefit entitlements. They also run the Veteran Peer Access Network, which pairs you with a trained veteran peer for help across housing, mental health, employment, and legal issues.
VME — Veterans in Media & Entertainment
A Los Angeles nonprofit, formerly Veterans in Film & Television, for veterans working in or breaking into film, TV, music, theater, and gaming. It passes along jobs, internships, and crew calls, works with more than 30 hiring partners across studios, networks, and agencies, and runs mentoring and workshops led by people already in the business. Open to veterans, active duty, Reserve, and Guard: a one-time $25 fee verifies your service, with no annual dues. Still on active duty? Join now — the VME Connect platform lets you reach veterans in the industry and build contacts before you ever get to LA.
Unhoused Veterans
Nationwide & statewide
Homeless Veterans Call Line — 877-424-3838
Start here. Free, confidential, answered 24/7 by trained counselors who connect you to the nearest VA. Family and friends can call on a veteran's behalf. Tap to dial: 877-424-3838.
VA Homeless Programs — What's Available
The full picture in one place: outreach, emergency and transitional beds through Grant and Per Diem, and permanent housing through HUD-VASH and SSVF. Useful for figuring out which door you need before you call.
HUD-VASH — Housing Vouchers for Veterans
A HUD rental voucher paired with VA case management. Ask your local VA Medical Center's homeless coordinator to start a referral, or go through the call line above.
SSVF — Rent, Deposits & Rapid Re-Housing
Short-term help through community partners: back rent, security deposits, utilities, moving costs, and case management, either to prevent a loss of housing or to get you back into one fast. For rapid re-housing, VA states that employment, income, criminal history, and sobriety are not prerequisites.
Veterans Justice Outreach
For veterans whose housing is blocked by court dates, an open case, or a record. VJO specialists link you to treatment and housing instead, and work with veterans treatment courts. Ask for the VJO specialist at your VA medical center.
San Diego County
Veterans Village of San Diego
Veterans helping veterans since 1981. Emergency and transitional housing, treatment and recovery programs, and employment services out of their Pacific Highway campus. Serves veterans of all eras.
Father Joe's Villages — Housing & Services
San Diego's largest homeless services provider, based downtown. Shelter, interim and permanent housing, meals, medical and dental care, and job training. Their housing navigators can connect veterans into VA programs.
Riverside County
U.S.VETS Inland Empire
March Veterans Village at March Air Reserve Base, serving Riverside and San Bernardino counties since 2003. Emergency, transitional, and permanent housing plus mental health care and career services for more than 400 veterans and their families.
Los Angeles County
VA Greater Los Angeles — Homeless Care
Shelter, food, and permanent housing help through more than a dozen community partners, anchored at the West LA campus. Their Veteran Peer Access Network is open to any veteran regardless of discharge status.
New Directions for Veterans
Founded in 1992 by formerly homeless Vietnam veterans. Four transitional housing programs and several permanent supportive housing sites across LA County, plus counseling, education, and job placement.
LA County Veteran Housing Directory
The county's list of veteran-specific housing: VASH-accepting buildings, permanent supportive housing sites, and the LAHSA veterans line. Useful when you know you need a unit and want to see what actually exists.
Fun Benefits
Nationwide & statewide
Vet Tix — Free Concert & Game Tickets
Currently serving, Guard, Reserve, veterans of all eras, and families of the fallen. Create an account, get your service verified, then request tickets. You pay only a small delivery fee.
National Parks — Free Military Pass
Veterans and Gold Star Families get a free lifetime pass. Active duty, Guard, Reserve, and dependents get a free annual pass. Covers 2,000+ federal sites — Joshua Tree, Cabrillo National Monument, the Santa Monica Mountains. Pick one up free at any park entrance with a CAC, VHIC, or DD-214, or order it from the USGS store.
Blue Star Museums — Free Summer Admission
Armed Forces Day through Labor Day each year: active duty, Guard, and Reserve plus up to five family members get in free at 2,000+ museums, zoos, and gardens. Balboa Park, many LA museums, and Inland Empire sites take part — check the map each spring to see who registered.
Guitars for Vets — Free Lessons & a Free Guitar
Ten weeks of free one-on-one guitar lessons for veterans working through PTSD and other service-related trauma, then a new acoustic guitar and accessories to keep. Around 130 chapters, including Long Beach and Victorville; most enroll through a VA case worker referral. In LA County, Veterans Guitar Alliance runs a similar free program out of Santa Clarita.
California State Parks — Distinguished Veteran Pass
Free day use, family camping, and boat use at over 100 state parks — Mount San Jacinto above Palm Springs, Lake Perris, Chino Hills, Silver Strand. For honorably discharged California residents with a combined rating of 50% or higher from wartime service, former POWs, and Medal of Honor recipients. Valid five years.
San Diego County
SeaWorld San Diego — Waves of Honor
One free admission per year for active duty, drilling reservists, and Guard, plus up to three dependents. Veterans usually get their own free-ticket window around Military Appreciation Month in May — register early, it closes fast.
San Diego Zoo & Safari Park
Active duty enter free every day of the year with a valid ID — no ticket needed. Spouses and dependents get 10% off a 1-Day Pass. Retirees and veterans go through the MWR/ITT office on base instead.
San Diego Museums — Free & Reduced
USS Midway, the San Diego Air & Space Museum, and the Veterans Museum at Balboa Park are free for active duty and reservists year-round. Veterans get a reduced rate.
More San Diego Military Deals
Del Mar racing — free general admission for active duty and dependents on race days — plus LEGOLAND, Navy Getaways cottages at Fiddler's Cove, base golf at Admiral Baker, and the ITT ticket windows, usually the cheapest tickets in the county.
Riverside County
VALOR & WAIVE — Free County Admissions
Riverside County's own thank-you programs. Free entrance to many county events and facilities for current and former military, their families, and guests, plus free parking at many county buildings.
Palm Springs Aerial Tramway — Military Days
Every July, free admission for active, former, and retired military with a valid ID, plus spouses of deployed members and military widows and widowers. Up to six guests get 25% off. The tram climbs to Mount San Jacinto State Park, which runs roughly 30 degrees cooler than the valley floor.
Los Angeles County
LA Fleet Week — Free, Memorial Day Weekend
Four days on the LA Waterfront in San Pedro: free public tours of active-duty Navy and Coast Guard ships, flyovers, equipment displays, and free trolley service. Ship tour tickets go through a same-day virtual queue. Free to everyone, no ID required.
Battleship IOWA Museum — San Pedro
The only battleship museum on the West Coast, now becoming the National Museum of the Surface Navy. Military and veterans get in free during LA Fleet Week; the rest of the year it's a military discount at the gate or through GOVX.
Aquarium of the Pacific — Long Beach
Free admission for veterans and military personnel with service ID on Veterans Day, advance reservations required. Year-round, buy discounted tickets ahead at any MWR/ITT office rather than paying at the window.
