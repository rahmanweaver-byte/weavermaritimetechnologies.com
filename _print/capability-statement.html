Company overview

Weaver Maritime Technologies is a veteran-owned company in San Diego supporting Navy ship repair and modernization. We plan, track and close out shipboard alterations for the fleet, from installation planning through compartment closeout, so supervisors, the prime and inspectors all work from the same picture.

Core competencies

01

SHIPALT installation planning

Alteration work sequenced against the availability schedule, engineering drawings and space access, so crews start on time and stay out of each other's way.

02

Compartment closeout & QA documentation

Space-by-space closeout, inspection records and objective quality evidence, assembled so sell-off goes through the first time.

03

Production & crew scheduling

Daily crew plans, manning and progress tracking that keep supervisors and the prime working from the same picture.

04

C5I installation support

Drawing cross-reference, cable and equipment tracking, and installation documentation for communications and combat systems work.

Differentiators

  • Deck-plate experience. Led by a production manager who has run Navy modernization work on the San Diego waterfront.
  • Records that hold up. Documentation built to the standard inspectors and primes check against, not reconstructed after the fact.
  • Status you can see. Progress reported by compartment and by alteration, so open items surface early.
