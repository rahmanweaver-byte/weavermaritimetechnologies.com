What we do

Ship repair and modernization support, start to finish.

We work alongside primes and ship repair teams on Navy availabilities in San Diego. Here is what we deliver, and what it solves for you.

  1. 01

    Alteration installation planning

    SHIPALT sequencing against the availability schedule, engineering drawings and space access.

    What you getCrews start on time and stay out of each other's way.

  2. 02

    Compartment closeout and quality

    Space-by-space closeout, inspection records and objective quality evidence.

    What you getSell-off goes through the first time.

  3. 03

    Production and crew scheduling

    Crew loading, daily plans and waterfront schedules that keep the trades moving.

    What you getSupervisors and the prime work from the same picture.

  4. 04

    Drawing and cable cross-reference

    Drawing, cable and configuration checks before installation begins.

    What you getProblems are caught before installation, not after it.

  5. 05

    QR-to-portal field tracking

    Technicians scan the QR code posted in each compartment to pull up its drawings, work items and checklists. Every update posts to our customer portal in real time.

    What you getProgress you can see as it happens, without waiting for a report.

Our focus

Every shipboard alteration starts with a plan and ends with a record. We keep both straight, from installation planning through compartment closeout, so supervisors, the prime and inspectors all work from the same picture.

How we work

Customer portal

From the deckplate to your desk, as it happens.

Our technicians update each compartment on a tablet, in the space. You follow the work in a secure portal: status, quality evidence and open items, without waiting for a report.

  1. 1

    Scan in the space

    A technician scans the QR code posted in the compartment, then updates its status, checklist and photos.

  2. 2

    See it live

    The update posts to your portal as soon as the tablet has coverage: closeout map, schedule and live feed.

  3. 3

    Review each space

    Every compartment has its checklist, quality evidence and history, ready for your walkthrough and sign-off.

  4. 4

    Decide in one click

    Problems found in the field arrive with a proposed fix. Approve it or ask a question, and the work keeps moving.

Founder & CEO

Rahman is a Marine Corps officer with more than 20 years of military service. He brings production management experience from Navy ship modernization programs in San Diego, including alteration documentation, compartment closeout and crew scheduling across multiple ships.

He holds a B.S. in Aerospace Engineering, with a concentration in Astronautics, from the University of Texas at Austin.

Raphael Weaver

Chief Technology Officer

Raphael brings more than 16 years of software and systems engineering, including a decade building mission systems for the Navy and Air Force as a defense contractor, from Aegis combat system simulation to F-16 radar warning software.

As Chief Technology Officer he leads technology at Weaver Maritime Technologies and the technical side of its government work: turning program requirements into delivered systems, and building the records and reporting that primes and inspectors rely on.
