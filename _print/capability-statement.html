Company Overview

Weaver Maritime Technologies, LLC is an SBA-certified service-disabled veteran-owned small business (SDVOSB) in San Diego supporting U.S. Navy ship repair and modernization. We plan, track and close out shipboard alterations so availabilities finish on schedule and pass sell-off the first time.

Core Competencies

Alteration installation planning

SHIPALT sequencing against schedules, drawings and space access.

Compartment closeout and quality

Space-by-space closeout, inspections and quality evidence.

Production and crew scheduling

Crew loading and waterfront schedules that keep trades moving.

Drawing and cable cross-reference

Drawing, cable and configuration checks before installation.

QR-to-portal field tracking

Every technician works from a secure tablet: scan the QR code posted in a compartment to pull up its drawings, work items and checklists. Each update posts to our customer portal in real time.

Differentiators

  • DDG and LPD modernization experience in San Diego
  • Led by a Marine Corps officer with 20+ years of service
  • Real-time customer portal fed straight from the deckplates
  • Based in San Diego, minutes from the waterfront
