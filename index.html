Weaver Maritime Technologies is a veteran-owned company supporting ship repair and modernization, from installation planning through compartment closeout.

Email us View capabilities

What we do

  • SHIPALT installation planning

    We sequence alteration work against the availability schedule, engineering drawings and space access, so crews start on time and stay out of each other's way.

  • Compartment closeout and QA documentation

    Space-by-space closeout, inspection records and objective quality evidence, assembled so sell-off goes through the first time.

  • Production and crew scheduling

    Daily crew plans, manning and progress tracking that keep supervisors and the prime working from the same picture.

  • C5I installation support

    Drawing cross-reference, cable and equipment tracking, and installation documentation for communications and combat systems work.

How we work

  • Deck-plate experience

    Led by a production manager who has run Navy modernization work on the San Diego waterfront.

  • Records that hold up

    Documentation built to the standard inspectors and primes check against, not reconstructed after the fact.

  • Status you can see

    Progress reported by compartment and by alteration, so open items surface early.
