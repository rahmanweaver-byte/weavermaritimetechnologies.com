What we do

Alteration support, from the first plan to the last closeout.

Four capabilities, built around how ship repair and modernization work actually runs on the San Diego waterfront. Each drawing below is live, so try it.

SHIPALT installation planning

  • 01
  • Planning

Drag an ALT along the hull, or tap a step

We sequence alteration work against the availability schedule, engineering drawings and space access, so crews start on time and stay out of each other's way.

Compartment closeout & QA

  • 02
  • Quality

Tap a space to send the inspector

Space-by-space closeout, inspection records and objective quality evidence, assembled so sell-off goes through the first time.

Production & crew scheduling

  • 03
  • Production

Turn the clock to change shifts

Daily crew plans, manning and progress tracking that keep supervisors and the prime working from the same picture.

C5I installation support

  • 04
  • Systems

Tap a cable to move it forward

Drawing cross-reference, cable and equipment tracking, and installation documentation for communications and combat systems work.

Our focus

Every shipboard alteration starts with a plan and ends with a record. We keep both straight, from installation planning through compartment closeout, so supervisors, the prime and inspectors all work from the same picture.
